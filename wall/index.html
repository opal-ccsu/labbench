The wall.

Every lab in a real statistics room has a wall of clippings. These are ours, in course order, each with the line it is pinned up for. Two artists, both of whom gave their work away under licenses that let a class use it: Allison Horst, whose monsters and dragons appear on the benches, and Randall Munroe of xkcd.
